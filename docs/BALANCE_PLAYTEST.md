# Toss the Turkeys balance baseline

## Mathematical baseline

The position picker normalises the configured weights before choosing each of the two independent landings. Under clear conditions, the current model produces approximately:

- 4.72 points per toss before banking decisions
- 21.12% chance of `Plucked It` on each toss
- 78.88% chance of a scoring result
- 4.73 tosses per pluck on average

This is intentionally volatile: most players should regularly face a meaningful bank-or-risk decision by their third or fourth toss.

Condition bonuses are deliberately smaller than the 4.72-point clear-weather baseline:

- Harvest Festival: +5 for each Jiblets or Wattle Wobble result
- Golden Hour: +2 when a toss scores
- Fox Alert: +5 when a toss scores
- Crosswind and Muddy Meadow alter motion and bounce, not outcome probabilities
- No condition bonus rescues `Plucked It`

## Real-group playtest protocol

Automated maths cannot measure whether a group laughs, understands a rule, or feels that a loss was fair. Run at least three sessions for each group size: 2, 3–4, and 5–8 players. Record:

- game duration and turns per player
- bank amount on every turn
- score lost on every pluck
- whether players understood the active condition without prompting
- how often players chose another toss at 10, 20, 30, and 40+ at-risk points
- perceived fairness from 1–5 after the game
- moments of confusion, delight, or downtime

Revisit the weights if pluck rate observed over 300+ tosses falls outside 18–24%. Revisit target scores if a 100-point game regularly exceeds 20 minutes for four players. Change a condition bonus only if players can identify that condition as too weak or dominant across multiple sessions.
